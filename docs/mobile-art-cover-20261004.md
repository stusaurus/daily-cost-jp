# Mobile editorial cover

The mobile hero is a single full-bleed artwork with live HTML copy and controls, rather than an image followed by a text panel. Desktop keeps the landscape image. The portrait source is served only below 600px, at 480w or 960w with intrinsic dimensions and high-priority loading inherited from the hero image.

Assets: `scripts/design/art/hero-mobile-480.webp` and `hero-mobile-960.webp`. Created with the built-in image generation tool; originals are not used as product evidence. Only resolution and WebP compression were applied after generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait mobile website editorial cover background, 2:3 portrait composition. Create a bold, exquisite surreal household still life for a Japanese daily essentials comparison brand, matching warm ivory, stone, sage glass and paper texture. A huge sculptural white toilet-paper roll at middle-right with a flowing fine tissue sheet arcing above it like soft sculpture, a small unlabelled clear sage glass bottle and linen on a matte stone ledge at lower-right. Soft diagonal morning window light, delicate natural shadows, photographic tactile paper and cloth, editorial museum-quality art, refined everyday materials, no plastic advertising look. COMPOSITION FOR HTML OVERLAY: top-left and left 65 percent of the upper 40 percent must be smooth warmly lit ivory negative space with no objects and no dark shadows, for dark Japanese heading; main paper sculpture occupies right half and middle band from 30 to 70 percent height, dramatically large and visible. Lowest 22 percent is a quiet warm ivory stone/linen surface with no busy details for HTML search and buttons. Entire picture feels one continuous art environment, no boxes or divisions. No text, no typography, no letters, no labels, no logos, no prices, no people, no watermarks, no neon, no gold, no marble, no decorative patterns. Avoid malformed roll openings and broken paper geometry.
